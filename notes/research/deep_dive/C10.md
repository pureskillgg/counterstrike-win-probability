## What differs from the brief

1. **Channels are files, not subfolders.** Each match folder has 43 extensionless files, one per channel (`bomb_action`, `player_vector`, ...). No subfolders exist in scope.
2. **Two stray files.** Day 05 has two files with a random 8-character suffix: `player_vector.334aDCEe` (match ce8d53e9-e3f1-4d71-a31b-9e3d7d1be812, 34,340,864 B) and `tick.7CFCBc03` (match dbd7f364-2732-44d5-866a-211822ad5d1c, 262,144 B). They look like leftover temp or duplicate files. They are excluded from the channel rows and estimates. Not verified, and nothing was deleted.
3. **The volume uses 1 MiB clusters.** E: is exFAT. GetDiskFreeSpaceW returns 2048 sectors x 512 B = 1,048,576 B per cluster. Every file therefore takes at least 1 MiB on disk. The "on-disk" columns round each file up to whole clusters. GetCompressedFileSizeW was tried first and returned the logical size, so it was not used.
4. **Matches per day are very uneven.** Day 08 has 1,135 of the 1,752 matches (64.8%). The brief's 219/day mean is pulled up by that day.
5. **Per-match sizes differ by day.** Days 01-05 average 26-37 MB logical per match. Days 06-08 average 7-10 MB. `player_vector` and `player_status` fall sharply from day 06. The October mean is therefore not a stable per-match size (see SENS 3 and 4 below).

## Judgement calls

- **Per-tick drops.** player_vector and player_status are named in the brief. I also judged tick, grenade_vector and world_item_vector as per-tick by name and size. player_inputs (15.6 KB per match) is kept as an event channel. If grenade_vector and world_item_vector are kept as event data, (b) becomes 6.16 GB logical and 137.78 GB on-disk.
- **Strays.** Excluded from channel rows and estimates.

## What the on-disk numbers mean

On this exFAT volume each channel file costs at least 1 MiB. The 38 event-channel files cost 38 MiB per match on disk, for 0.93 MB of content. So dropping the per-tick channels saves much less on disk than in logical bytes: (a) to (b) cuts logical GB by about 94%, but on-disk GB by about 30%. Packing each match into fewer files, or using a volume with smaller clusters, would bring on-disk close to logical. Neither option was measured here.

## Limits

- Estimates assume September per-match sizes match October. Pooled player_vector differs about 3.7x between days 01-05 and 06-08, so the real figure could fall anywhere in the SENS 3-4 range.
- The 1 MiB cluster was read from the volume once, at run time. Per-file on-disk sizes are computed from st_size and that cluster size, not read from the filesystem per file.
- Days 09-11 exist under E:\csds_final\csds\2026\10 but are outside the requested scope and were not read.
