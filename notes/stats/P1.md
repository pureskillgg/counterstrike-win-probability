# P1: pistol-round statistics (2026 tome, 1 Hz freeze-end sample)

Scope: pistol rounds are round 1 and round 13 (MR12). Side codes: 2 = T, 3 = CT. Intervals are 95% match-cluster bootstrap percentile intervals: 1000 draws, seed 0, matches resampled with replacement (rounds are not resampled independently). Universe: 1,752 matches for the tome section, 150 matches for the 1 Hz section.

Scripts: `scripts\stats\P1_verify_mapping.py`, `P1_round_outcomes.py`, `P1_pistol_sample.py`, `P1_pistol_types.py`, `P1_budget_check.py` (plus `P1_probe_schema.py`, `P1_probe_sample.py`). Logs: `logs\stats\P1_*.log`.

Nothing here is causal. Momentum, armor and money results are associations and are not adjusted for team strength, map, or economy beyond what is shown.

## 5. Cross-checks and sample caveats

- Sample pistol CT win vs tome. Round 1: sample 50.0% (n=144), tome 49.7% (n=1,752), tome with player_count==10 49.4% (n=1,559). Round 13: sample 42.1% (n=126), tome 48.3% (n=1,697), tome with player_count==10 47.3% (n=1,504). The round 13 sample gap (about 6 points, ~1.4 SE) is not significant but is noted.
- Sample T pistol win rate: 53.7% (145 / 270 side-rounds), versus tome T win 50.3% (R1) and 51.7% (R13).
- Armor-based and money-based results are associations. Armor and equipment are bought with money, so the armor and equipment results likely reflect spending.

## 6. Cannot check or not done

- The bake-off kept set (1,521 matches) was not applied. The tome section uses all 1,752 matches as instructed.
- Pistol sample rounds are a filtered subset (see section 0). Selection beyond the round-13 coverage counts is not quantified.
- Pre-buy (start-of-freeze) money is not in the sample. The equipment identity suggests money is post-buy cash, but the 800 starting value and the 200 default-pistol value in equipment_value_calc are inferred, not verified against the extract definition.
- Helmet prices and the halftime armor reset mechanism are not verified from the data. The data pattern is consistent with a reset.
- inv_secondary = 0 (no secondary) appears for 5 players in pistol first snapshots; not explained.
- Within-team type comparisons have very small n for non-default types (CIs span zero).
- No team identity is tracked across matches, so team strength is not controlled.
- No meridian provenance or checkpoint call was made (outside this subtask's write scope).
