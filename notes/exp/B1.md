# B1: logit-average blend of existing predictions

Script: scripts\exp\B1_blend.py. Log: logs\exp\B1_blend.log. Outputs: data\viz\pt_preds\B1_val.npy and B1_test.npy (float64). One row appended to data\viz\pt_results.jsonl by E.report("B1_blend").

## Method
- Candidates: every <name>_val.npy / <name>_test.npy pair in pt_preds with lengths 162,065 (val) and 404,833 (test). All were finite.
- Blend: p = sigmoid(sum_k w_k * logit(p_k)), w_k >= 0, sum to 1. Weights fit on VALIDATION by minimising mean logistic loss. Solved by projected gradient with backtracking onto the simplex (numpy only, no scipy). The objective is convex in w. KKT check passed: active-weight gradients agree to about 1e-8, and inactive gradients are higher.
- Excluded: X10_temp, X10_iso, X10_bintemp. X10_calib.py fit these calibrations on the validation predictions, so their validation scores are in-sample and would be unfairly favoured. Also dropped as exact duplicates: X1 and X10 (both identical to MONO_mono_all).
- Candidates used (10): X5, MONO_mono_all, MONO_mono_none, MONO_mono_alive_only, MONO_mono_score_only, X2, X4, X11, X11_s1, X11_s2.

## Blend weights (fit on validation)
X5 0.883552, MONO_mono_all 0.093070, MONO_mono_none 0.023378. The other seven candidates have weight 0.

## Blend results
- Validation log loss: 0.465788.
- Test (E.report "B1_blend"): log loss 0.477254, match-cluster 95% CI [0.468472, 0.485871]. Round-weighted 0.463697. Brier 0.161649. AUC 0.838742. ECE10 0.010277.
- Versus CPU XGBoost baseline (0.4916): -0.014396, CI [-0.018115, -0.010667].

## Blend versus best single (paired, match-cluster bootstrap, 1000 resamples)
- Test: blend minus X5 = -0.000257, paired 95% CI [-0.000563, -0.0000004].
- The interval excludes zero, but only just: its upper bound is about -4e-7. The point estimate is about 0.00026 nats, roughly 0.05% of the loss.
- Validation gain over X5 was smaller, -0.000105.
- Read: the blend is marginally better than X5 by a statistically distinguishable but tiny margin. It is not a meaningful improvement.

## Caveats
- Weights and the candidate set were chosen on validation, and the gain over the best single model is tiny. Treat this as a near tie.
- MONO_mono_all = X1 = X10 is the same GPU XGBoost run, so including its duplicates would have double-counted it.
- Compute: the blend is numpy on about 160k x 10 logits, which runs in seconds on CPU. GPU acceleration would not help, so the GPU env interpreter was used only for its numpy and the helper. No CUDA work was done.
- Nothing was committed. No .env, meridian.toml or E: files were read or written.
