# 2024 vs 2026 csds schema comparison

Read-only comparison of csds channel manifests and parquet schemas. No data rows were read, and nothing under E: was written.

## Verdict

2024 data cannot use the 2026 channel list unchanged. The channel lists differ, the 2024 parquet files carry an extra `__index_level_0__` column, and several shared columns change integer width, signedness, or type between years. The highest-risk items are the `player_status` and `player_vector` integer narrowings: 2024 int64 data read into the int8, int16 and int32 types that some 2026 matches declare. Next are the bool/int changes and the parquet columns that 2026 has and the 2024 files do not.

## Sample and method

- 2024: `2024\02\02` and `2024\02\12` (tree `csds_tail_recovered_20261011`), 5 match folders each.
- 2026: `2026\10\01` through `2026\10\08`, 5 match folders each (40 total).
- A folder qualifies when its `csds` manifest is gzip JSON that parses and every channel it declares has a parquet file of that name. Picks are a seeded shuffle, first 5 that qualify per day. Every candidate that was checked qualified.
- Parquet types come from `pyarrow.parquet.read_schema` (footer only). The manifest `type` is the csds logical type (for example `int`, `int64`, `string`). Columns with origin `merged` or `calculated` have no `type` key, so manifest type comparisons skip them.
- Risk scores read each 2024 type into the 2026 declared type. Higher is riskier. Int narrowing scores 100 plus the bit drop (+15 if signedness also changes). Bool vs non-bool scores 95. Same-width sign change scores 90. Float to int scores 85. Dictionary encoding scores 40, or 75 with a different value type. Float64 to float32 scores 70. Null-typed columns score 25. Widening scores 10. Identical scores 0.
- Coverage limit: 10 of 1,192 2024 folders and 40 of 1,752 2026 folders (Oct 1-8) were read. Other columns or types may vary in the unsampled folders.

Sample counts: 2024-02-02: 5 of 188 match folders qualify; 2024-02-12: 5 of 1004 match folders qualify; 2026-10-01: 5 of 67 match folders qualify; 2026-10-02: 5 of 65 match folders qualify; 2026-10-03: 5 of 100 match folders qualify; 2026-10-04: 5 of 109 match folders qualify; 2026-10-05: 5 of 83 match folders qualify; 2026-10-06: 5 of 104 match folders qualify; 2026-10-07: 5 of 89 match folders qualify; 2026-10-08: 5 of 1135 match folders qualify

Schemas read: 2024 = 300 parquet files across 10 folders; 2026 = 1680 across 40 folders.

## Summary

- Channels declared in both years: 28. 2024 only: 2. 2026 only: 14.
- Column-name differences on shared channels: 75 grouped rows (section 2). 19 are parquet columns that 2026 has and no sampled 2024 file has (HIGH). Column presence also varies within each year (QA).
- Shared parquet columns whose type set differs between years: 85. By risk: HIGH (overflow) 34, HIGH 30, MEDIUM-HIGH 15, MEDIUM 2, LOW 4.
- `__index_level_0__` (a pandas index column) appears in the 2024 parquet of 27 of 28 shared channels and in no 2026 parquet.
- 2024 parquet schema read failures in the sample: 0 of 300 files.
