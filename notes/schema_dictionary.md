- round_end.winner_team_code and score_update.team_code are side-based. Scores are cumulative per side across halftime (no reset).
- team_change row labels: 10 rows labeled round 12 at tick 64005. The tick table puts tick 64005 in round 13. Join by tick, not by round, for that table.
- player_status and player_vector only have rows for alive players. There are no health = 0 rows, and no rows at or after a player's death tick. Of the 1,360,560 (tick, player) cells, 994,274 have a row and the 366,286 without one are all explained by an earlier death of that player in the same round (checked). Ticks with fewer than 10 rows are post-death periods, not missing data.
