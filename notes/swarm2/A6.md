# A6: labels and win codes (both tomes)

Scope: (1) `ct_win = (winner_team_code == 3)` in the 2026 and 2024 tomes; (2) `win_reason_code` against `win_reason_message` on every round; (3) the 2024 win codes 8 and 9 (dominant in 2024) against bomb events, score fields and deaths. Everything was recomputed from the parquet with column projection, one channel at a time, well under 3 GB. Nothing was written under E:. Nothing was edited in `bakeoff.py`, `extract_state_1hz.py`, or any existing output. The model was not run.

Sources: `tomes\2024-02-g\tome\csds` (4,435 matches, 83,527 round_end rows) and `tomes\2026-10-01_to_08\tome\csds` (1,752 matches, 35,992 rows).

## Result in one paragraph

The label definition is fine in both eras, and every round's win code agrees with its message. The 2024 problem is in the round_end rows themselves, and it has two parts. (a) For matches dated 2024-02-12 onward, many bomb-ended rounds carry the elimination codes 8/9 instead of 1/7, because the round_end row is logged about 429 ticks (6.7 s, ASSUMPTION 64 ticks/s) after the decisive event. (b) In matches with no event-coded round, the round_end winner is inverted at rounds 9, 13, 25 and 28. The 499 bomb-verified side mismatches decompose exactly as r13 434 + r28 31 + r25 24 + r9 10, and every one of them sits in those matches. The bomb events agree with the score fields in every case that can be checked, so the round_end winner is the outlier. This is the open round-13 defect, and it changes labels in the 2024 cross-period test.

## Assumptions (all marked ASSUMPTION where used)

- ASSUMPTION A-a: win codes follow the canonical enum, with names taken from the message strings: 1 Target_Bombed (T), 7 Bomb_Defused (CT), 8 CTs_Win (CT), 9 Terrorists_Win (T), 11 (CT), 12 Target_Saved (CT), 13 (T), 17 Terrorists_Surrender (CT), 18 CTs_Surrender (T). This matches `scripts/model/feature_validation.py` (WIN_REASON, CT=3, T=2) and `notes/research/csds_docs_facts.md` F3.
- ASSUMPTION A-b: 64 ticks per second (2026 `header.tick_rate` = 64; 2024 not separately checked). 429 ticks = 6.7 s.
- ASSUMPTION A-c: score-derived side = `round_state` `round_freeze_end` score delta between round r and r+1, no swap. Validated on 2026 against `score_update`: 34,200 of 34,200 rounds with a +1 score_update row agree with round_end.
- ASSUMPTION A-d: an elimination round has 5 distinct victims per side. Rosters from `player_info` are not exactly 5 in about 10% of 2026 elimination rounds, so the death check is used only comparatively.
- ASSUMPTION A-e: duplicate matches are identical round_end sequences (fingerprint). This is close to the bake-off rule in `bakeoff.py` L12-19. 2024: 229 groups, 301 non-first copies, 4,134 kept. 2026: 47 groups, 52 copies, 1,700 kept.
- ASSUMPTION A-f: "elimination-only match" = a match with no round_end row coded 1 or 7. This is a proxy for the rebuilt version, not a proven flag. It is not a perfect classifier (see A6-5).

### A6-1 ct_win definition and winner domain: refuted, none

- Label in use: `scripts/model/bakeoff.py` L230 `label_ct_win = int(rr["winner"] == CT)` and L334 `y = label_ct_win`; CT = 3 and T = 2 from `scripts/model/feature_validation.py` L38.
- 2026 (`logs/swarm2/A6_label_audit.log`, section [1]): 35,992 rows; winner 3 = 18,865 (ct_share 0.524144); winner 2 = 17,127; 0 values outside {2, 3}; 0 null; 0 duplicate (match_key, round) keys.
- 2024 raw: 83,527 rows; winner 3 = 42,421 (ct_share 0.507872); winner 2 = 41,106; 0 outside {2, 3}; 0 null; 1 duplicate key with identical winner and code.
- Duplicates: 2024 deduplicated set has ct_share 0.50825 (77,510 rows, `logs/swarm2/A6_final_numbers.log`). The 2024 cross-period script already removes duplicate matches (`scripts/model/sufficiency/cross_period.py` L268-280). Duplicates do not move the label share.
- The definition is right. The 2024 winner values are wrong in specific rounds, which is A6-5.

### A6-2 win_reason_code vs win_reason_message, every round: refuted, none

- 2024: 83,527 rows, 9 distinct (code, message) pairs, one message per code, 0 disagreements with the canonical enum, 0 code/side disagreements.
- 2026: 35,992 rows, the same 9 pairs, 0 disagreements, 0 code/side disagreements.
- Code counts 2024: 1 = 2,130; 7 = 4,009; 8 = 37,644; 9 = 38,929; 11 = 12; 12 = 719; 13 = 8; 17 = 37; 18 = 39. Codes 8+9 = 91.7% of rows.
- Code counts 2026: 1 = 2,566; 7 = 4,337; 8 = 14,006; 9 = 14,547; 11 = 2; 12 = 497; 13 = 2; 17 = 23; 18 = 12. Codes 8+9 = 79.3%.
- The 2024 concentration of 8/9 is a symptom of A6-4. It is not a message/code pairing error.
- Scripts: `scripts/swarm2/A6_code_message_xtab.py` (log `logs/swarm2/A6_code_message_xtab.log`), `scripts/swarm2/A6_label_audit.py` (log `logs/swarm2/A6_label_audit.log`).

### A6-3 D6_winreason_check.py swaps 17 and 18: supported, medium

- `scripts/swarm2/D6_winreason_check.py` L11-12: `T_REASONS = {1, 9, 13, 17}` and `CT_REASONS = {7, 8, 11, 12, 18}`. Code 17 (Terrorists_Surrender) is a CT win and code 18 (CTs_Surrender) is a T win. The script has them reversed.
- Its logged disagreements are exactly that swap: 2024 = 37 (code 17 with winner 3) + 39 (code 18 with winner 2) = 76 (`logs/swarm2/D6_winreason_check.log` line 2). 2026 = 23 + 12 = 35 (same log, 2026 section).
- Corrected count from the data: 0 disagreements in both eras (A6-2).
- Fix: in D6_winreason_check.py set `T_REASONS = {1, 9, 13, 18}` and `CT_REASONS = {7, 8, 11, 12, 17}`, fix the docstring line 2, and re-run. Not edited here, because it is not an A6 file.

### A6-4 2024 bomb-ended rounds coded 8/9 from late round_end rows: supported, medium

Numbers (raw, `logs/swarm2/A6_window_bomb_split.log`, `logs/swarm2/A6_bomb_tick_probe.log`):
- Days 01-07 (before the 2024-02-12 entry): defused 2,026 rounds, all coded 7 (100%). Exploded 1,404 rounds: coded 1 = 1,017 (72.4%), coded 9 = 386 (27.5%), coded 18 = 1. Side mismatches 0.
- Days 12-25: defused 7,194 rounds: coded 7 = 1,983 (27.6%), coded 8 = 4,851 (67.4%), coded 9 = 360 (5.0%). Exploded 5,296 rounds: coded 1 = 1,113 (21.0%), coded 9 = 4,044 (76.4%), coded 8 = 139 (2.6%).
- Timing: every code-8 bomb round in days 12-25 (4,990 rounds) has its bomb event before round_end, with median offset -429 ticks and no round at offset 0 (share at offset 0 = 0.0). For the defused rounds coded 8 the offset range is -525 to -193 ticks. Code-9 bomb rounds (4,404) have median -429 (offset 0 in 0.07%). Every code-7 and code-1 round has offset 0 (100%).
- Deduplicated 2024 bomb-ended rounds: 14,635, coded 1/7 = 5,784 (39.5%), coded 8/9 = 8,850 (60.5%). 2026: 7,170, coded 1/7 = 6,661 (92.9%), coded 8/9 = 509 (7.1%, all exploded then coded 9 with the explosion logged after round_end, offset +301 median).
- Death evidence does not separate code 8 from code 7 in 2024. The loser side is fully dead (5 victims) in 84% of defused rounds coded 8 and in 78% coded 7. In 2026 it is 91% for code 7. This fits a late-logged reason, not a different round state.
- Matches dated 2024-02-12 onward mix two versions. Matches with at least one event-coded round (25% of deduplicated days 12-25 matches, 783 of 3,168) keep offset-0 codes. The rest are elimination-only. The docs (`notes/spec/csgo_and_docs_digest.md` L22 and L144; `notes/research/csds_docs_facts.md` F6) describe the rebuilt rows as "codes only 8 and 9". The data contradicts that: days 12-25 still have 3,096 bomb rows coded 1/7 and about 400 rows coded 11/12/13/17/18.
- Impact on labels: none directly, since the label is the winner and the win code is not in the feature list (`scripts/model/bakeoff.py` FEATS). It does invalidate any 2024 analysis that uses reason codes, and the "8 and 9 dominant" pattern itself.
- Fix: do not use 2024 `win_reason_code` or `win_reason_message` as a reason or outcome label. Take the bomb outcome from the bomb_state event rows, using only offset-0 rounds as the reliable subset.

### A6-5 2024 round_end winner inverted at rounds 9, 13, 25, 28: supported, high

This is the open round-13 defect. Its mechanism is the winner side, not a bomb misattribution.

Bomb-side mismatch, all 2024 rows (`logs/swarm2/A6_threeway_probe.log` P2, `logs/swarm2/A6_swap_round_probe.log`):
- Round 13: 434 of 813 bomb rows mismatch (53.4%). Days 01-07: 0 of 195. Days 12-25: 434 of 618.
- Round 25: 24 of 51 bomb rows mismatch, all 24 in elimination-only matches. Round 28: 31 of 65, all 31 in elimination-only matches. Round 9: 10 of 801 bomb rows mismatch, all 10 in elimination-only matches (77 such rows in those matches).
- Sum = 434 + 24 + 31 + 10 = 499 = every bomb-side mismatch in the tome. Every one sits in an elimination-only match. Within elimination-only matches, the bomb-row mismatch rate at round 13 is 434 of 444 (97.7%).
- Reconciliation with the open-defect figures: with `player_count == 20` the numbers reproduce exactly: 434 of 605 round-13 rows in days 12-25, 0 of 180 in days 01-07, and 41 non-13/25 mismatches (`logs/swarm2/A6_reconcile_player20.log`).

Score-derived side (no swap; A-c), raw:
- Round 13: 2,051 of 4,001 score-scored rows disagree with round_end (51.3%). Round 25: 124 of 246. Round 9: 292 of 4,314. Round 28: 105 of 201. Round 27: 127 of 127 (boundary artifact, see A6-8).
- Where a bomb side exists, the score-derived side equals the bomb side in 799 of 799 round-13 rows (and 51 of 51 round-25 rows). Round_end equals the bomb side in only 379 of 813.
- Elimination-only versus event matches, round 13: elimination-only 2,187 deduplicated rows, 1,866 inverted (85.3%). Event-coded matches: 1,533 rows, 0 inverted. Days 12-25 only: 1,866 of 2,095 inverted (89.1%) against 0 of 748.
- Not a bomb-event artifact: round-13 rows with no bomb event are inverted at the same rate (1,621 of 3,202, 50.6%) (`logs/swarm2/A6_r13_match_probe.log`).
- Within a match, round 13 and round 25 are inverted together (124 matches with both inverted, 122 with neither).
- Deduplicated 2024 (`logs/swarm2/A6_final_numbers.log`): round 13 inverted 1,866 of 3,720 score-scored rows (50.2%); round 25 inverted 114 of 229 (49.8%). Total 1,980 rounds, which is 2.55% of 77,510 deduplicated round_end rows.
- Other rounds are fine. Non-13/25 score disagreements fall only on rounds 9, 27 and 28. Round-level agreement elsewhere is perfect (2024 and 2026 round-by-round tables in `logs/swarm2/A6_swap_round_probe.log`).
- 2026 is clean at the same rounds: round 13 has 0 of 1,649 score disagreements and 0 of 308 bomb mismatches; round 25 has 0 of 163 and 0 of 47; round 9 has 0 of 1,731 and 0 of 387.
- Why the cross-period result is affected: `notes/model/sufficiency/cross_period.md` L5 reads "2024 round_end winners UNVERIFIED". This audit answers that. The round_end winner is wrong at these rounds in the elimination-only matches, and right elsewhere (99.25% agreement with score deltas, 99.7% with bomb side). The 2024 log loss 0.50441 (`notes/model/sufficiency/cross_period.json` L247) therefore includes wrong labels for about 2.5% of rounds, concentrated in swap rounds. The size of the effect on the log loss was not measured.
- Possible second exposure (not checked): if any 2024 feature is derived from the winner (as the 2026 score-lead feature is per `notes/model/sufficiency/score_lead_fix.json`), it inherits the same inversion.
- Fix: for the 2024 rows flagged as elimination-only, set ct_win at rounds 9, 13, 25 and 28 from the bomb side where a bomb event exists (defused = 3, exploded = 2). Otherwise use the no-swap freeze-end score delta (A-c). Drop rounds where neither is available. Re-run `scripts/model/sufficiency/cross_period.py` with the corrected 2024 labels and report both versions. Until then, the 2024 verdict should state that these labels are known to be wrong.

### A6-6 2024 round 1 absent from round_end: supported, medium

- `logs/swarm2/A6_round1_probe.log`: 1,957 of 4,435 raw matches (44.1%) have no round_end row for round 1. Deduplicated: 1,859 of 4,134 (45.0%).
- All 984 matches dated 01-07 lack round 1. In days 12-25, 973 of 3,451 lack it.
- Round_start has round 1 in 4,434 of 4,435 matches, so the round exists in the source. The round_state freeze-end row for round 1 is missing in exactly the same 1,957 matches, and round_state has no round_end row for round 1 in any 2024 match.
- Max round, contiguity and gaps: every affected match runs 2..N with no interior gaps (`logs/swarm2/A6_label_audit.log` P1, 0 matches with a gap). 2026: 0 matches missing round 1.
- Impact: the 2024 test set has no pistol-round labels in 44% of matches, and none at all from days 01-07. This shifts the composition of the cross-period set. The label values of the rounds that are present are not affected.
- Fix: exclude round 1 from 2024 evaluation and say so, or rebuild round 1 from round_start and round_state. Document the gap.

### A6-7 2024 round_end logged after the decisive event: supported, medium

- Deduplicated 2024, code 8/9 rounds with a death (70,948): round_end later than the last death in 41,976 (59.2%), equal in 26,113 (36.8%), earlier in 2,859 (4.0%). 2026 (27,727): later in 0, equal in 26,298 (94.8%), earlier in 1,429 (5.2%).
- Deduplicated bomb-ended rounds, 2024 (14,635): round_end later than the bomb event in 7,870 (53.8%), equal in 5,788 (39.5%), earlier in 977 (6.7%). 2026 (7,170): later in 0, equal in 6,661 (92.9%), earlier in 509 (7.1%).
- Median gap, 2024 code-8 rounds: round_end minus last death = 429 ticks (about 6.7 s, ASSUMPTION A-b), 95th percentile 1,204 ticks. Code-8 bomb rounds in days 12-25: bomb event minus round_end = -429 ticks (median). Source: `logs/swarm2/A6_bomb_tick_probe.log`.
- Impact: the live window (round_freeze_end to round_end, per `scripts/model/bakeoff.py` section 2 and 4) for 2024 runs about 6.7 s past the decisive event in the affected rounds. No model feature was re-run. The effect on the 2024 states is not measured. This is the same timing that produces the 8/9 codes in A6-4.
- Fix: for 2024, end the live window at the last death or bomb event tick (ASSUMPTION: the gap is logging delay, not live play), or record the extension. Re-run the 2024 build and compare.

### A6-8 2026 OT swap-boundary disagreements: refuted, none

- 106 2026 rounds (27: 86; 33: 12; 39: 6; 45: 2) are the rounds just before an overtime side swap (swap positions as listed in `notes/spec/feature_spec.md` section 3). Each has two +1 score_update rows (one per side). The round_end tick equals the first row's tick (106 of 106), and the second row lands 544 ticks later.
- The score-delta check fails on these rounds (106 of 32,639 resolved, 0.32%). The check fails on round 27 in both eras, with 2024 round 27 at 127 of 127 disagreements.
- The label is not contradicted: round 27 bomb rows in 2026 (34) all agree with round_end (0 mismatches, `logs/swarm2/A6_swap_round_probe.log`). Rounds 33, 39 and 45 were not checked against bomb rows (the probe stops at round 30).
- Conclusion: a boundary artifact in the freeze-end score fields, not a label defect. Fix only the check: exclude the swap-boundary rounds from the score-delta test.

### A6-9 legacy_code undocumented; docs F6 contradicted: supported, low

- 2024 only. 435 rows have legacy_code != 0 (1: 235; 2: 133; 3: 67). All are win code 7, winner 3, and bomb defused. None of the 499 bomb-side mismatch rows carries a legacy value.
- Per day: 226 rows in days 01-07 and 209 in days 12-24. `notes/research/csds_docs_facts.md` F6 says rebuilt rows have `legacy_code` = 0 and `player_count` = 20. The data shows nonzero legacy values in the rebuilt window, and player_count = 20 in only 94-100% of days 12-25 rows (2,258 of 65,436 rows are not 20).
- No model script reads legacy_code (`scripts/swarm2/A2_round_probe.py` and `scripts/swarm2/D5_01_explore.py` read it, no model script does).
- Fix: document the meaning of legacy_code or drop it from the 2024 tome. Correct F6 and the csgo_and_docs_digest entries at L22 and L144.

## What was not checked

- The model was not run, so the effect of A6-5 and A6-7 on the 0.50441 log loss is not measured.
- The bake-off's 2024 kept set was not reconstructed. Its filters are written for the 2026 tome. Counts are raw or fingerprint-deduplicated round_end rows, not bake-off states.
- The elimination-only flag (A-f) is a proxy. Some elimination-only matches are not inverted (e.g. 16-round-format rows at round 13). The death check (A-d) is comparative only.
- Round 1 score checks are impossible in 2024: no freeze-end row exists for round 1 in any match without round_end round 1, and round 1 freeze-end is absent in all 984 matches dated 01-07.
- The 2024 header has no max_rounds, so match format was inferred from the last round (`fmt` in `scripts/swarm2/A6_swap_round_probe.py`). That inference is approximate.
- No external ground truth was used beyond bomb events and score fields.
