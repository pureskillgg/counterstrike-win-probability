# D2: is winner_team_code attached to team identity rather than side?

Agent D2, swarm2. Scope: hypothesis D2 only (2024 round-13 side vs bomb defect). No edits to bakeoff.py, extract_state_1hz.py, existing outputs, .pixi, .env or meridian.toml. No git commits. Interpreter: `G:\pureskill\csds-cs2\.pixi\envs\default\python.exe -I -X utf8`.

## Verdict

- **As stated (winner code follows team identity in general): refuted.** 2026 has 1 flipped round out of 31,141 rounds with a side truth. In 2024, outside flagged matches, the recorded code is side-attached (14 flips in 41,497 rounds of 1-12, 6 in 22,549 of 14-24).
- **For the defect rows only: supported.** Of 2,137 flipped 2024 rounds, the recorded code equals a team-attached code of the winning team: the static identity (team X = code 2) at rounds 13 and 25, and the winner's side in round 27 at round 28. Rounds 13 and 25 match static identity (1,837/1,837 and 112/112 with side data), round 28 matches the pre-swap side (113/113).
- **A team-attached conversion is not a fix.** Applied to every round-13 swap row, it would invert all 1,409 2026 round-13 swap rows (which are correct now), and on 2024 round 13 it is right only where the flag already says the row is flipped. The flag must come from side-attached data.
- **Fix:** rebuild 2024 round winners from side-attached sources (round_state freeze-end scores tracked across the swap, checked against bomb outcomes and eliminations). Do not use `winner_team_code` or `win_reason_code` for flagged 2024 rounds. Do not use a winner-derived score lead for 2024: it is wrong in 12,591 rounds across 1,702 matches, starting at round 14.
- The bake-off's primary result (2026 only) is not affected. `cross_period.py` (2026 model, 2024 test labels from `winner_team_code`) is affected, so the 0.50441 cross-period figure must be recomputed.

## Test

Round window: `round_freeze_end` tick to `round_end` tick. In this tome `round_start` is not a window boundary: in 2024 its tick is after `round_end` in 79,063 of 79,063 rounds (median +20 ticks); in 2026 it never is (median -5,394).

Team X: the five players on code 2 in the first valid round of each match (valid = 10 players, 5 on code 2, 5 on code 3). `sx` = side of team X in round r, from `player_info.team_code`. Swap detection: `sx != sx_prev`.

Side truth T (side-attached; ASSUMPTION that `round_state.t_score`/`ct_score` are side-attached, checked below):
- team-tracked round_state score delta across the swap (freeze-end r to freeze-end r+1): winner is team X if its score rose by 1 and Y's did not, mapped back to a side.
- Cross-checks: bomb outcome in the round window (exploded = T, defused = CT), and elimination (all five players of one side dead, victim side from team membership).

Readings of the recorded code R (= `winner_team_code`):
- side reading: R == T.
- flipped: R == 5 - T.
- static team reading: R = identity of the winning team (2 if X won, else 3).
- lag-1 team reading: R = the winning team's side in round r-1.

Filters: 2024 `round_end.player_count == 20` (78,596 rows; 1 duplicate (match, round) pair, first kept); 2026 `player_count == 10` (32,041 rows). ASSUMPTION: these counts mark a full 5v5 round, as in `e2_side_check_r13.py` (2024) and `bakeoff.py` (2026).

Win-reason mapping (ASSUMPTION, standard CS round-end reasons): T = {1, 9, 13, 17}, CT = {7, 8, 11, 12, 18}. All reason codes in both tomes are mapped.

Coverage: T known for 68,392 of 78,595 rounds in 2024 (87.0%) and 31,141 of 32,041 in 2026 (97.2%). Source agreement: s_score vs s_bomb 12,920 of 12,920 equal (2024), 6,512 of 6,512 (2026); s_score vs R 61,119 of 63,141 (2024), 29,176 of 29,176 (2026).
