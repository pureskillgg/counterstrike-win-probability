# D5: round-numbering hypothesis for the 2024 round-13 side/bomb defect

Agent: D5. Scope: 2024 tome only (G:\pureskill\csds-cs2\tomes\2024-02-g\tome\csds). Reads only; no existing file edited; no git; no E: writes. Intermediate parquet files are in the scratchpad (C:), not in the project.

## Verdict

**Refuted as stated.** Round 13 is not mislabelled by a shift in round numbering. No gaps, no duplicate rounds, and no extra rounds sit around round 13 in the affected matches, and the bomb, freeze-end, and round-end timelines line up on the same labels.

**The defect is a value inversion, not an index error.** In "regime A" matches (the per-match marker `round_announce_match_start` is present), the `round_end` row of the first round after a side-field swap (the halftime) has its `winner_team_code` and `win_reason_code` inverted. The inversion is confirmed by two independent truths: the bomb outcome, and the freeze-end score step, which agree with each other. For MR12 matches the first post-halftime round is round 13; for MR15 (first-to-8) matches it is round 9; in overtime it is rounds 25 and 28.

**The e2 count of 434 undercounts the damage.** It only sees rounds with a bomb outcome. Using the score step and the last-victim side, roughly 2,040 of the 2,478 regime-A matches have an inverted label at the first post-halftime round (about 1,800 at round 13 and about 240 at round 9). Regime B (1,957 matches) has none.

## Definitions and assumptions

- ASSUMPTION (affected set): the e2 definition. A match is affected if a round-13 bomb outcome (bomb_defused or bomb_exploded, from bomb_state) disagrees with `round_end.winner_team_code` (player_count == 20). Reproduced exactly: 434 matches, 434 mismatches. Mismatch count by round: 13: 434; 25: 24; 28: 31; 9: 10 (D5_02 log).
- Groups: aff_r13 = 434 matches; ctrl_late = 3,017 matches (days 12-25, not affected); early = 984 matches (days 01-07).
- ASSUMPTION (regime): regime A = `round_announce_match_start` count >= 1 per match (2,478 matches = 434 aff + 2,044 ctrl_late); regime B = absent (1,957 = 973 ctrl_late + 984 early). The marker matches `round_end` label 1 presence exactly (0 mismatches).
- ASSUMPTION (score fields): `round_state` t_score / ct_score at round_freeze_end are side-attached and swap at halftime (confirmed on example matches and by the side-swap classification below). A boundary r -> r+1 is a side swap when t(r+1) = ct(r) + [w_r = 3] and ct(r+1) = t(r) + [w_r = 2]. Ties make some boundaries ambiguous; ambiguous ones are excluded.
- ASSUMPTION (independent truth): a round with no bomb_planted row is decided by its last victim (player_death.player_team_code at max tick); the implied winner is the opposite side. Noise floor about 3-8% (labels 2-12, both regimes); it rises to 12-38% at labels 16-24 in all groups including early, so it is used only as corroboration.
- Header final scores (`t_starters_score_final`, `ct_starters_score_final`) were not used. They do not reproduce round totals even in clean matches. Example: match csds/2024/02/01/m_partial: header 14 + 7 = 21; freeze-end scores plus round winners give 18 rounds (final 5-13).

## What would fix the labels

The hypothesis (round numbering shift) would need a re-indexing fix (re-assign each round_end row to the freeze-end interval containing it). That is not needed: the timelines already agree (T2, T4).

The real fix is value correction for regime-A matches:

1. Re-derive `winner_team_code` for each (match, label) from independent evidence, in priority order: (a) bomb outcome if a bomb_defused / bomb_exploded row exists (defused -> 3, exploded -> 2); (b) freeze-end score step r -> r+1, using the halftime-aware mapping (a side swap at r -> r+1 maps the fields crosswise); (c) last-victim side for no-bomb rounds. Flag rounds where (a), (b), (c) disagree or are unavailable.
2. Recompute `win_reason_code` / `win_reason_message` from the corrected winner and bomb outcome (bomb reasons 1 and 7 where a bomb outcome exists). Do not use the existing reason codes as evidence.
3. Restore round 1 for regime B matches: when round_end label 1 is absent, take the round-1 winner from freeze-end scores at round 2 (scores start at 0-0).
4. Add per-match fields to the tome or the bake-off split: regime (round_announce_match_start count) and halftime format (MR15 if the last-round-half announcement is at label 7; MR12 at label 11).
5. Keep the bake-off score lead on round_state freeze-end scores. Do not switch it to round_end-derived leads until step 1 is applied.
6. Validate: on regime B, the corrected winners must reproduce round_end in all usable rounds (currently 100% of usable steps for labels 2-11; the 8 -> 9 swap in MR15 needs the swap mapping). On regime A, the corrected winners must match bomb and last-victim truth where those exist. Then re-run the bake-off cross-period test. Not done here.

Expected scale of the fix (ASSUMPTION: usable-step estimate, not a full re-derivation): about 2,040 inverted labels in about 2,040 regime-A matches, one inverted site per match: about 1,800 at round 13 (MR12; about 430 of them in the affected set, the rest bomb-less rounds) and about 240 at round 9 (MR15). Round 25 and 28 inversions occur in the same matches as the round-13 inversions (control: 11 + 64 usable; affected: 21 + 4 + 1 + 10 usable with the 13 pattern), so they add labels but not matches.

## Caveats and not checked

- The cause of the inversion (parser or team-mapping state) is not identified. The claim that the round_end row is the wrong one rests on two independent truths (bomb outcome, score step) plus last-victim evidence.
- MR15 identification is inferred from the announcement label, the final round range, and the swap pattern. The header has no direct round-limit field that I checked.
- The 2026 tome was not checked for regime markers or MR15 matches. The bake-off's zero mismatches for 2026 (e2 log) are consistent with no regime-A inversions there, but this is not verified here.
- Cross-period log loss was not re-run; the size of the effect on the 2024 test log loss (0.50441) was not measured.
- The 8 -> 9 "swap" in MR15 matches was not checked against round_state overall; it is a format halftime, not a pause, restart, or knife event. No pause or restart event signature was found in those matches (D5_18).
- Death-based truth is noisy late in the round count (T6); the score step is the stronger reference where it is valid.
