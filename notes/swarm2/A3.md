# A3: dedupe audit (bake-off rule, 2026 tome; 2024 cross-check)

Scope: the bake-off duplicate rule in `scripts/model/bakeoff.py` (grouping at lines 636-666, copy
counts at 714-725, split assertions at 326-328), its counts, whether it drops legitimate matches,
and whether it misses duplicates across the train/val/test boundary. Read-only. `bakeoff.py` was
imported only to reproduce run 1 (`A3_leak_effect.py`); it was not run as a script, and no existing
output (`logs/`, `notes/model/`, `models/`) was written. The 2024 tome was checked as well, because
the cross-period result and the round-13 analysis read it and the bake-off does not.

## Summary

- The 2026 rule is correct as implemented. Its counts reproduce exactly, it drops no distinct match,
  it misses no duplicate in the 2026 tome, and the final split contains no copy on two sides.
- Outside the 2026 bake-off, two findings change reported numbers. The 2024 round-13 counts include
  duplicate copies: 434 mismatches become 391 after removing them (the rate stays about 72%). And the
  2024 day groupings use folder dates that agree with `match_date` for only 33.6% of matches; by
  `match_date` the round-13 mismatch is a step at 2024-02-07, not a gradient from day 12.
- The MODEL_CARD credits +0.0031 of XGBoost test log loss to deduplication. Measured, dedupe moves
  it by about 0.0001. The rest is the re-split.

## Details and evidence

### A3-1 (none, refuted): 2026 counts reproduce

`logs/swarm2/A3_dedupe_audit.log`, all 21 CHECK lines OK, 0 failed.
- 1,752 headers; 43 dropped for a missing channel; 1,709 kept.
- Round filters on kept matches: 35,502 rounds; player_count != 10: 3,663; disconnect or takeover: 1,767;
  not 5v5 at start: 476. Survivors: 29,596 rounds in 1,568 matches.
- Signature groups among kept matches: 46 groups, 97 matches (sizes: 41 pairs, 5 triples).
- Copies dropped: 51 (97 - 46). Of these, 47 had surviving rounds (913 rounds removed); 4 had none.
  The "47 removed" in the bake-off log and the "removes 51 matches" in MODEL_CARD line 49 are both correct.
- Result: 1,521 matches, 28,683 rounds (matches `logs/bakeoff.log`).

### A3-2 (none, refuted): the lowest-key rule keeps the content

- 42 groups have at least one surviving round. In all 42 the lowest-key copy holds the rounds.
- Within every group the surviving round count is identical across members (46 of 46).
- Latent risk only: the rule would fail if copies ever differed in channel presence. None do. Fix
  if desired: keep the member with the most surviving rounds, and assert that no signature group
  remains among the final kept matches.

### A3-3 (none, refuted): no distinct match is dropped

- Per-match hashes of nine channels (round_end, round_start, round_state, player_death, player_info,
  bomb_state, team_change, player_disconnect, bot_takeover) are identical within all 46 groups.
- max_rounds and header final scores agree in all 46 groups.
- Smallest signature in a group: 111 event rows. No group has fewer than 50.
- Only `match_date` differs: median 4 s, maximum 5,747 s (about 1.6 h); 13 groups have zero spread.
- Limit: 9 of the tome's channel folders were hashed, plus the header fields above. Not all channels were hashed.
- Coarse keys over-merge, so the exact rule is needed. Grouping by (max_rounds, header scores, winner
  sequence) gives 74 groups; 28 of them are not one signature. Their median round-end tick gaps are
  1,154 to 24,805 ticks, so they are different games (`A3_dedupe_audit.log`, section 5b).

### A3-4 (none, refuted): no 2026 duplicate is missed

Three independent tests over all 1,752 matches (`A3_dedupe_audit.log`, sections 5, 5c, 5d):
- Tick-tolerant pairs (same round, same winner, round_end within 64 ticks): 207 pairs share at least
  three rounds. 57 of them share one signature (all at least 8 rounds). The other 150 share at most
  4 rounds, which is coincidence level. Non-grouped pairs with at least 8 tolerant rounds: 0.
- Constant-offset copies (same winners, round ticks shifted by one fixed amount): 0.
- Exact death/round-end overlap: 57 pairs; 56 are grouped; the 57th is a pair of two matches both
  dropped by the channel filter (2026-10-02, identical signature, missing team_change).

### A3-5 (none, refuted): no copy straddles the final split

- `A3_dedupe_audit.log`, section 6: final primary split 1,095 / 122 / 304 matches; signature groups
  straddling sides: 0 (primary and chronological); non-grouped near pairs straddling: 0.
- The dedupe runs before `make_splits`, so the final split is built on the deduplicated set.

### A3-6 (low, supported): run 1 did leak across the split

- Run 1 split (1,568 matches): 1,129 / 125 / 314. 17 signature groups straddle the primary split;
  0 straddle the chronological split (matches the bake-off's own check, `bakeoff.py` lines 646-666).
- 12 of 314 test matches have an exact twin in train or validation: 220 of 5,838 test rounds and
  1,823 of 47,603 test states (`A3_leak_effect.log`).
- The current run removes this (A3-5). Informational.

### A3-7 (medium, supported): MODEL_CARD credits the wrong cause

- `notes/model/MODEL_CARD.md` line 92: "Effect of deduplication: ... Removing the duplicates raised it to
  0.4366 (+0.0031)." The +0.0031 is run 1 (0.43353) to this run (0.43660).
- Reproduction: `A3_leak_effect.py` reproduces run 1 exactly (validation 0.44219, test 0.43353).
- Experiment: remove the 11 train/validation copies of the 12 exposed test matches and refit XGBoost with
  the same settings. Overall test log loss 0.43353 becomes 0.43347 (-0.00006). Exposed matches go from
  0.43407 to 0.43650 (+0.00243, copies were helping them). The other 302 matches move by -0.00016, which
  is an estimate of retraining noise (best iteration 270 becomes 321).
- So the dedupe effect on test log loss is about 0.0001. The +0.0031 comes mostly from the re-split: the test
  set changes from 314 to 304 different matches. `bakeoff_results.md` line 124 says this correctly; MODEL_CARD does not.
- Fix: reword MODEL_CARD line 92 to "run-to-run change, including a re-split; dedupe itself moves test log loss by about 0.0001 (measured)".

### A3-8 (medium, supported): the 2024 tome is not deduplicated by the bake-off

- `bakeoff.py` reads only `tomes/2026-10-01_to_08`. It does not touch 2024.
- `notes/swarm2/D9.md` line 65 says "Duplicates are a separate data issue (already handled by bakeoff)."
  That is wrong for 2024.
- 2024 tome (4,435 matches, `A3_dedupe_2024.log`): 226 exact-signature groups covering 522 matches; 296
  redundant copies (6.7%). Sizes: 178 pairs, 28 triples, 18 quads, 2 quintuples.
- `scripts/model/sufficiency/cross_period.py` lines 268-281 apply their own lowest-key rule to 2024:
  202 groups and 271 copies on its kept set (`notes/model/sufficiency/cross_period.md` line 69). So the
  cross-period test set is deduplicated.
- `scripts/swarm/e2_side_check_r13.py` applies no deduplication. That is why A3-9 applies.
- Cross-tome: 0 exact-signature pairs and 0 tolerant pairs with at least 8 rounds between the 2024 and 2026 tomes.
- Fix: correct D9 line 65. Apply one shared dedupe to every 2024 analysis (or add it to e2).

### A3-9 (high, supported): round-13 counts in 2024 include copies

Definition as in `e2_side_check_r13.py` (round_end.player_count == 20, joined to bomb_defused or bomb_exploded;
bomb_side 3 if defused else 2; mismatch = bomb_side != winner). `A3_dedupe_2024_impact.log`, `A3_r13_copy_removed_by_date.log`:
- All round-13 rows with an outcome: 785; mismatches 434. This reproduces the e2 figure.
- Copies removed (301: 296 exact plus 5 distinct copies behind the near pairs in A3-10): 67 copy rows,
  43 of them mismatches. Remaining: 718 rows, 391 mismatches.
- Folder days 12-25 (the e2 framing): 605 rows / 434 mismatches becomes 538 / 391 (71.7% becomes 72.7%).
- Conclusion unchanged: copies do not explain the defect. The count does change. Report 391 of 538 or both.
- Copies agree with their lowest-key copy on mismatch status in all 1,264 compared rows (all rounds with an outcome), so no group is internally inconsistent.

### A3-10 (low, supported): seven near-duplicate pairs missed by the exact rule

- `A3_dedupe_2024_impact.log`: 7 pairs of 2024 matches share at least 8 rounds within 64 ticks with the same winner
  but do not share one signature. Their round_end ticks are identical (diff 0 in every common round), winners identical,
  and death sets identical. In every pair the smaller `player_info` set is contained in the larger (for example 215 within 230).
  The exact rule ignores that difference.
- Distinct non-lowest copies: 5 (two copies are each paired twice). Round rows with an outcome in these copies (all rounds, not only round 13): 16, with 0 mismatches.
- Not checked: whether these copies survive `cross_period.py`'s relaxed filters (ASSUMPTION: they do, since the exact rule does not remove them).
- Fix: add the tolerant test (round_end ticks within 64, same winners, at least 8 rounds) to the dedupe, or compare round_end plus death sets only.

### A3-11 (high, supported): the 2024 day framing depends on the date field

The defect description groups by "matches dated 2024-02-12..25". That grouping is the key folder
(`csds/2024/02/DD/`), not `match_date`.
- Folder and `match_date` agree (full calendar date) for 1,492 of 4,435 matches (33.6%) (`A3_keyday_check.log`).
  The 2026 tome agrees for 1,730 of 1,752 (`bakeoff_results.md` line 246), so the 2026 chronological split is not affected.
- Folder 2024-02-12 holds 1,004 matches; only 75 have `match_date` 2024-02-12.
- Round-13 mismatches, copies kept (`A3_r13_by_matchdate.log`), by full `match_date`:
  2023-12: 0 of 2; 2024-01: 0 of 202; 2024-02-01..06: 0 of 140; 2024-02-07: 33 of 35; 2024-02-08..11: 108 of 108;
  2024-02-12..25: 293 of 298.
- By folder: the reported "0 of 180 in 02-01..07" and the 51%-to-100% gradient over days 12-25 both reproduce
  (12: 94/185, 13: 34/54, 14: 20/30, 15: 17/19, 16: 41/42, 17: 16/16, 25: 28/28).
- Which field is the played date is UNVERIFIED (the bake-off makes the same caveat for 2026). Until it is
  verified, do not describe a day pattern in 2024 without naming the field. By `match_date`, the mismatch
  is a step at 2024-02-07, not a gradient.
- Scope note: this is outside the dedupe, but it changes a reported description of the defect.

### A3-12 (low, supported): docstring wording

`bakeoff.py` lines 12-15 say the duplicate rule "runs after the round filters and before the splits." The
groups are formed on channel-kept matches before the round filters (lines 636-645); only the removal is applied
to survivors after them (line 660). The effect is nil (A3-2). Fix the wording.

### A3-13 (low, supported): the fingerprint figure needs a footnote

`bakeoff_results.md` line 245 and `bakeoff.py` line 1004 say "round-fingerprint check ... found 47 groups." The
signature rule gives 46. The extra fingerprint group is two matches (m_70c0bb0c, m_4fee9278, both 2026-10-02) that the
channel filter drops (both lack team_change) and that have identical signatures. Add that sentence to the note.
