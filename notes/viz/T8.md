# T8: Smokes (detonation positions, smokes per round, lifetime)

Source: 2026-10-01 to 08 tome, 1,752 matches. Script: `scripts/viz/T8.py`. Outputs: `data/viz/T8.json`, `data/viz/T8.svg` (two panels in one 800x450 SVG).

## Readout

Across the 1,752 matches, a clean 5v5 round (round_end player_count == 10) averages 3.99 smoke detonations over rounds 1-24 (123,422 smokes in 30,953 rounds). Smokes per round follow the economy pattern: pistol rounds 1 and 13 average about 1.30 (95% CI 1.24-1.34 and 1.25-1.34), the second round after each pistol averages 2.49 (round 2) and 2.60 (round 14), and rounds 3-12 and 15-18 sit between 4.1 and 4.7. Rounds 22-24 reach 4.9-5.6, but those rounds have only 284-648 clean rounds each, so treat them as less certain. On de_mirage, the most common map (346 matches), there are 28,032 detonations in 6,870 clean rounds; thrown by T 16,444 (58.7%), by CT 11,562 (41.2%), and 26 with unknown side. Observed lifetime from expiry-minus-detonation, paired by entity id, is exactly 1,412 ticks (22.06 s) for all 126,806 paired expiries, with zero spread; this looks like a fixed timer in the parser output rather than a measured dissipation.

## Lifetime (smokegrenade_expired minus smokegrenade_detonate, paired by entity_id)

- 126,806 expiry events, all paired (backward as-of join on match_key, round, entity_id). Mean = median = min = max = 22.0625 s (1,412 ticks at 64 tick/s). SD = 0.
- 11,683 detonations have no expiry. All 11,683 fall where detonation tick + 1,412 is after round_end, so the round ended before the expiry would fire.
- 0 duplicate pairings. Match-cluster bootstrap CI for the mean is degenerate: [22.0625, 22.0625] s.

## Caveats and what was not checked

- "Smokes thrown" is proxied by grenade_state smokegrenade_detonate rows (138,489 total). There is no throw event in the data used, so smokes that never detonate are not counted.
- 632 detonations land after their round's round_end tick (573 in clean rounds; 534 were thrown before round end). They are kept, assigned to the round grenade_state gives them (0.46% of all detonations). The effect of dropping them on the per-round means was not tested.
- tick_throw is unreliable: 95 detonations show a throw-to-detonation gap over 60 s. It is not used for counting.
- 149 matches have header max_rounds = 16. Their rounds have only 2-4 players, so they contribute no clean 5v5 rounds. The sensitivity difference is 0.0 by construction.
- Rounds 19-24 have fewer clean rounds (1,072 down to 284) because only longer matches reach them. Their means may be selected, and their CIs are wider.
- Round-number means pool both sides and both halves; side-swap effects are not separated. Pistol rounds 1 and 13 are taken from the task facts, not checked against money data.
- entity_id is reused within a round for 21 detonation pairs. These are distinct smokes at different ticks and positions, so every detonation row is counted.
- Bootstrap: resampling is by match (1,752 clusters, 1,000 resamples, seed 0, percentile 2.5-97.5). The lifetime interval is degenerate, as noted above.
- The SVG layout was checked from its element counts (27 rects, 3 point paths with 11,562 / 16,444 / 26 segments, 24 bars, 56 text labels) and its coordinates. It was not rendered as an image.
- The 1 Hz sample was not used.
