# S8: CT win rate by decile of team money difference

Files: `scripts/viz/S8.py`, `data/viz/S8.json`, `data/viz/S8.svg`. Run: `G:\pureskill\csds-cs2\.pixi\envs\default\python.exe -I -X utf8 scripts\viz\S8.py`.

## Readout

In 2,402 non-pistol rounds across 150 matches (2026-10, days 01-07), the CT round win rate rises with the CT-minus-T team money difference at freeze end. The most T-favoured decile (median CT minus T of -$23.0k) gives CT a 42.7% win rate (95% match-cluster CI 36.6% to 50.6%). The top two deciles (median +$16.8k and +$28.6k) sit near 64% (CIs 57.8% to 70.7% and 57.3% to 70.3%). The curve is not strictly monotone: decile 3 (52.5%) sits above decile 4 (45.4%), and their intervals overlap. Money difference alone gives AUC 0.582 (95% CI 0.557 to 0.610). Equipment difference alone gives AUC 0.683 (95% CI 0.661 to 0.705), so equipment separates round outcomes better than cash at freeze end. These are descriptive associations in one sample of 150 matches, not causal effects.

## Checks

- Money and equipment sums from the 1 Hz freeze-end snapshot match `round_buy_types` exactly (max absolute difference 0) for all 2,402 analysis rounds.
- Pistol flag from round number (1 and 13) agrees with `round_buy_types.is_pistol` on every row that has it.
- Counts are non-negative and sum to 2,402. Intervals are ordered. Every point estimate sits inside its own interval.
- The sklearn `roc_auc_score` values match the weighted-AUC point estimates (0.581942 and 0.683313).

## Caveats

1. Descriptive, not causal. Freeze-end money depends on earlier round results (loss bonus, carried money), and equipment partly reflects the buy decision. Confounding by previous rounds and round type is likely.
2. The sample is incomplete at the match level. 44 of 150 matches have gaps in round numbers. 24 matches have no round 13 and 6 have no round 1 in the clean set. The highest round per match ranges from 2 to 29. I did not check whether these gaps come from the clean filter, and if they do, the selection could bias the decile distribution. The tomes were not used.
3. Decile edges are fixed on the full sample and not re-estimated per bootstrap resample. Equal-count deciles are unequal in size (238 to 242) because of ties.
4. The curve is not strictly monotone (decile 3 above decile 4). Interpret the shape as a rise from about 43% to about 64%, not as a step function.
5. The AUCs are in-sample point estimates. The train and test splits are pooled and no model is fitted. Intervals come from 150 match clusters, so they are approximate.
6. The SVG layout was checked from its coordinates and text dump, not rendered to an image.
