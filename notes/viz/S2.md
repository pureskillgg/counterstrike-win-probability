# S2: Mean freeze-end team equipment value by round number (CT vs T)

Files: `scripts/viz/S2.py`, `data/viz/S2.json`, `data/viz/S2.svg`. Source: `data/state_sample` only (1 Hz sample, 150 matches, 2,673 rounds).

## Readout

Across 2,622 clean 5v5 rounds in rounds 1 to 24 (150 matches), freeze-end team equipment follows the MR12 buy cycle. Pistol rounds 1 and 13 sit near $3,000 to $3,500 per side (round 1: CT 3,258, T 3,047; round 13: CT 3,525, T 3,229). Rounds 2 and 14, the round after each pistol, are partial-buy rounds near $11,000 per side. In the full-buy rounds (3 to 12 and 16 to 24), CT runs at about $18,700 to $26,300 and T at about $16,700 to $21,800, and CT's point estimate is above T's in every round except 14 and 15 (in those two, T is higher by 751 and 183, with overlapping intervals, so no conclusion is drawn). The point gap in CT minus T ranges from 1,961 (round 3) to 5,336 (round 17) across rounds 3 to 24 outside 14 and 15, and the two 95% intervals do not overlap in rounds 3 to 13, 16 to 19, 21 and 22. Pooled over rounds 1 to 24, CT averages 19,290 (95% CI 18,937 to 19,676) and T averages 16,635 (16,354 to 16,956). Intervals widen sharply after round 20 because only 51 down to 11 rounds remain in the sample for rounds 21 to 24, so those values are noisy.

## Method

- Freeze-end state: the first snapshot per (match_key, round), taken as `seconds_since_live == 0`. Every one of the 2,673 sample rounds has a second-0 snapshot.
- Team value: sum of `equipment_value_calc` over alive players with `team_code` 3 (CT) or 2 (T). Each side has 5 alive players in every plotted round.
- Side rule: each line is the side the team is on in that round. The lines are by side, not by team identity.
- Round-number mean: mean over matches of the side sum for that round number (one round per match per round number).
- Bootstrap: matches are the clusters; 1,000 resamples; percentile 2.5 and 97.5 with linear interpolation; `boot_se` in the JSON. Bands on the chart are pointwise, not simultaneous.
- Checks (all passed, in `checks` in the JSON): no interval-order violations; every point estimate inside its interval; no negative means; no zero side sums; the per-side sums match `round_buy_types.ct_equipment` and `t_equipment` exactly for all 2,622 plotted rounds that appear in that table.

## Caveats

1. Excluded round: match `csds/2026/10/07/m_7392c5c6d537a57c2e735e98/csds`, round 16. T had 4 alive players at freeze end (CT had 5). The buy-type table also omits this round. Excluding it is why n is 2,622 and not 2,623.
2. Overtime rounds (round_number 25 and up, 50 rounds in the sample) are not plotted, per the 1 to 24 scope.
3. Side, not team: sides swap after round 12, so rounds 1 to 12 and 13 to 24 are different team halves. A team-level series would need a team identity mapping, which I did not build.
4. `equipment_value_calc` is a calculated loadout value, not cash. A comment in `scripts/stats/A1_armor_stats.py` says it already includes armour, helmet and defuser costs. I did not re-verify that claim.
5. The sample is 150 matches from 2026-10 days 01 to 07 at 1 Hz. It includes both train (2,185 rounds) and test (488 rounds) splits, pooled here because the chart is descriptive. I did not check whether these 150 matches are a random draw from the 1,752 tome matches, and I did not use the tomes. The intervals reflect only sampling variation across these 150 matches.
6. Per-round n falls from 144 (round 1) to 11 (round 24). Intervals after round 20 are very wide, and round 24 in particular rests on 11 rounds.
7. CT minus T is a point difference. "Intervals do not overlap" is a descriptive comparison, not a formal test of the difference.
8. I checked the SVG by reading its markup, coordinates and text. I did not render it in a browser.
9. The 1 Hz sample is clean-round data; any round the upstream filter dropped is not in the sample and is not recoverable here.
