# T10: Momentum, next-round win rate after win and loss streaks

Files: `scripts/viz/T10.py` (analysis and SVG), `data/viz/T10.json` (series and key numbers), `data/viz/T10.svg` (800 x 450 chart).

## Readout

In the 1 Hz sample (150 matches, 2,623 regulation rounds, 4,116 eligible team-round targets), a team that won its last round won the next round 61.1% of the time (95% match-cluster CI 58.1 to 64.5; n = 846). After two straight wins the figure is 65.8% (62.1 to 69.6; n = 497). A team that lost its last round won the next one 38.9% of the time (35.5 to 41.9; n = 846), and 34.2% after two straight losses (30.4 to 37.9; n = 497). The gap between the win line and the loss line is about 22 points at k = 1 (16.2 to 29.0) and between 23 and 32 points at k = 2 to 5+. The step happens at the first round: beyond k = 1 both lines are flat within their intervals, so streak length adds little on top of a one-round streak. This is momentum-like serial dependence in the descriptive sense only. Team strength, economy (loss bonus and carry-over money), score state and side or map effects all move with streaks and are not controlled, so the gap is not a causal momentum effect.

Sensitivity (exclude only pistol rounds 1 and 13, so round 14 is kept): win line 61.9 / 66.2 / 64.1 / 62.3 / 63.9% (n = 906 / 524 / 309 / 183 / 249); loss line 38.1 / 33.8 / 35.9 / 37.7 / 36.1%. The conclusions do not change.

## How the team was tracked across the halftime swap

Team A is the five players on CT at the first observed round with five CT players in each match (the freeze-end roster). Team B is the other five. In every round, team A's side is whichever side (CT or T) has the larger roster overlap with team A. The team's round outcome is then whether its side won. Team identity therefore follows the players through the swap, not the side. Checks: no ambiguous rounds and no matches without an anchor. Side changes occurred 124 times at the 12 to 13 halftime, 9 times at the OT 27 to 28 swap, and 8 times across a missing round, with no other changes. Independent check: the pre-round score columns (`ct_score + t_score = round - 1` for every regulation round) agree with each team's cumulative round wins in all 2,132 round checks from gap-free matches, with zero mismatches for either team.

## Definitions and caveats

- Streak k is the run of consecutive regulation rounds, ending in round r, that the team won (win line) or lost (loss line). A missing round breaks the run. A run cut by a gap is kept only at 5+. Gap-cut runs below 5 dropped 216 eligible states.
- The 5+ bin means five or more consecutive rounds. Exact k = 5 is not shown separately.
- Excluded targets: pistol rounds 1 and 13, plus round 14. I read "rounds right after the halftime swap" as 13 and 14, since 13 is already a pistol. The sensitivity block excludes only 1 and 13.
- Overtime rounds 25 to 29 (50 rounds, 11 matches) are not used.
- Each round enters twice: as the winner's win streak and as the loser's loss streak, with the same k. This is why the n values match exactly for the two lines. The lines are not independent samples, and the gap is a winner-versus-loser contrast on the same rounds.
- One round has four T players in its freeze-end snapshot. It is kept, because team mapping uses roster overlap and it is consistent with `ct_win`.
- Sample: 150 matches from the 1 Hz sample (days 01 to 07 of 2026-10), not the full 1,752-match tome set. Representativeness is not checked.
- Not checked: the tome channels were not used. The 1 Hz `ct_win` values agree with `round_buy_types` for every round. Visual layout was checked only in a headless Chrome render, not on the slide itself.
- Intervals are percentile bootstrap intervals over 150 clusters. They were not validated against an analytic or alternative interval.
