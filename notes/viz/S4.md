# S4: Higher-equipment-side win rate by round class

**Readout.** In the 1 Hz sample (150 matches, 2026-10 days 01-07), the side with more freeze-end equipment value won 63.3% of the 2,658 decided rounds (95% match-cluster bootstrap interval 61.5-65.3%). The rate depends mainly on the buy matchup. Where one side bought full against an eco or force buy, the higher-equipment side won clearly more often: anti_eco 80.7% (n = 264) and force_vs_eco 80.6% (n = 217), and full_vs_force 62.3% (n = 1,130, interval 59.6-65.1%). Where both sides bought the same way, the edge is smaller: full_vs_full 55.4% (51.1-59.4, n = 601), force_vs_force 57.1% (49.4-64.2, n = 177), and pistol 58.2% (52.4-64.4, n = 261), where the median equipment gap is only 675. eco_vs_eco has 8 rounds, so its 62.5% (25.0-100.0) cannot be read. In the three classes where the higher-equipment side is the bigger buy by construction, these rates describe how often the bigger buy wins, not the effect of having more equipment.

## Caveats and assumptions

- **Round count differs from the brief.** `round_buy_types.parquet` has 2,672 rounds. `rounds.parquet` has 2,673. The missing round is round 16 of match `csds/2026/10/07/22e9c3e7-a333-4ebc-a51c-19ebbcc13ef1/csds`. It is present in `rounds.parquet` and in the 1 Hz sample, but absent from `round_buy_types`. I did not check the upstream cause. The chart uses the 2,672 rounds in `round_buy_types`.
- **Ties excluded.** 14 rounds have equal equipment value and no higher side (pistol 9, force_vs_force 2, full_vs_full 3). They are removed from both the numerator and the denominator.
- **eco_vs_eco is too small.** It has 8 rounds from 8 matches. The bar is drawn dashed and the interval spans 25% to 100%. Do not read anything into it.
- **Class labels fix the higher side in three classes.** I compared buy-type pair counts with higher-side counts, and they match exactly. In anti_eco, force_vs_eco and full_vs_force, the higher-equipment side is always the side with the bigger buy. Those rates therefore describe matchup outcomes and are not estimates of an equipment advantage.
- **Pistol higher side is a small gap.** Pistol rounds are eco vs eco. The median absolute equipment gap is 675 (90th percentile 1,700), so the higher side there is close to arbitrary. Treat 58.2% with caution.
- **Sample scope.** Only the 150-match, 2026-10 days 01-07 1 Hz sample was used. The 1,752-match tomes were not used. These results do not generalise beyond this sample without a check.
- **Split column ignored.** The `split` column (train 2,184 / test 488 in `round_buy_types`) was not used. All rows enter the descriptive rates.
- **Bootstrap checks.** No resample had zero decided rounds for any class. The cluster SEs are close to simple binomial SEs for the large classes, for example full_vs_force 0.0146 vs about 0.0144, so clustering changes the intervals only a little here.
- **Descriptive only.** There is no multiple-comparison adjustment, and the intervals are per class, not simultaneous.
- **Not checked.** I did not render the SVG to a screenshot. The layout was checked from parsed coordinates and text. The 14 tie rounds and the missing round 16 were not traced upstream.
