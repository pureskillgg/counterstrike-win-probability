# T2: Kills by weapon, top 15 (CT / T attacker split)

Files: `scripts/viz/T2.py` (computes everything), `data/viz/T2.json` (series and key numbers), `data/viz/T2.svg` (800 x 450 slide chart).

## Readout

Across 230,051 kills in 32,041 clean 5v5 rounds (1,592 matches, 2026-10-01 to 08), AK-47 is the top weapon at 34.5% of all kills (79,338 kills; 95% match-bootstrap interval 34.1% to 34.8%), and it is mostly a T weapon (75.4% of its kills had a T attacker). M4A1-S (15.8%) and M4A4 (6.9%) are almost entirely CT weapons (92.6% CT each), USP-S (5.1%) is 94.1% CT, and Glock-18 (4.5%, 98.6% T), Galil AR (3.3%, 92.0% T) and MAC-10 (2.8%, 96.8% T) are T-side. The AWP (9.2%) splits 63.1% CT and 36.9% T. The top 15 together cover 94.4% of kills. The 95% share intervals are 0.12 to 0.75 percentage points wide; they come from resampling matches (1,000 resamples, seed 0), not rounds.

## Data and method

- **Weapon column:** `basic_player_death.weapon_name` (68 raw values).
- **Name cleaning:** lower-cased and stripped; `m4a1_silencer_off` folded into `m4a1_silencer` and `usp_silencer_off` into `usp_silencer`; `knife`, `knife_t`, `knife_*` and `bayonet` folded into `knife`. Display labels (for example `m4a1` as M4A4, `m4a1_silencer` as M4A1-S) are my mapping from CS2 internal names. I did not check them against a table in the repo.
- **Rows:** 253,480 `player_death` rows, all joined to `basic_round_end` on (match_key, round) with no unmatched rows. The clean population is round_end `player_count == 10`: 32,041 rounds in 1,592 matches (234,027 death rows). The other 160 matches have no 5v5 round (mostly 4- and 11-player rounds) and are not in the chart. Round_end has 35,992 rounds with no duplicate keys.
- **Exclusions:** world deaths (weapon `world`, `worldent`, `planted_c4` or `trigger_hurt`, or attacker null or 65535) 3,378; suicides (attacker_id = player_id) 65; team kills (same side, not self) 533. Kept kills: 230,051.
- **Attacker side:** `attacker_team_code` (2 = T, 3 = CT) for 230,137 rows; the same round's slot-to-team map for 447 rows where that code was null. No row needed the opposite-of-victim inference. The slot-to-team map had 0 conflicts within rounds.
- **Interval:** match-cluster bootstrap, 1,000 resamples of the 1,592 matches with replacement, `random.Random(0)`, percentile 2.5 and 97.5 with linear interpolation. Shares use the resampled all-weapon total as the denominator.

## Caveats and checks

1. **Post-round kills.** 896 kept kills (0.4%) occur strictly after the round_end tick. The spec does not exclude them, so they are in the chart. Dropping them changes no top-15 share by more than 0.03 points (AK-47 34.49% to 34.46%). The 1 Hz sample's round_end ticks matched the tome round_end ticks in all 2,673 shared rounds.
2. **Sample.** The chart uses the full 2026 tome population (1,592 clean-5v5 matches), not the 1 Hz sample (2,673 rounds). The sample was used only for the tick consistency check. The n label on the chart is kills, clean rounds and matches from the tomes.
3. **Denominator.** Shares are of all kept kills, including knives, grenades, Zeus and fire, not only the top 15.
4. **Cross-check.** An independent polars pipeline that uses only row-level team codes gives AK-47 79,125 kills against 79,338 here. The gap is the 447 round-slot side values and the victim sides that the primary pipeline resolves; it is explained, not a discrepancy.
5. **Overtime.** Rounds 25 to 48 with `player_count == 10` are included, per the spec's clean-5v5 rule.
6. **Visual check not done.** The SVG was checked by parsing it (well-formed XML, viewBox `0 0 800 450`, 33 rect and 59 text elements) and by reading its coordinates (bars from x = 170, axis 0 to 100k, 15 rows at 20 px). It was not rendered to an image, so the visual spacing is unverified.
7. **Not checked.** Individual kills were not traced back to the demo, and the display-name mapping is unverified.
