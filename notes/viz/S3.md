# S3: Freeze-end team equipment, non-pistol vs pistol (1,000 USD bins)

Files: `scripts\viz\S3.py`, `data\viz\S3.json`, `data\viz\S3.svg`.

## Readout

In the 150-match 1 Hz sample (2,672 rounds; 5,344 team-rounds with both sides pooled), most non-pistol freeze-end team equipment sums are high: 54.2% of the 4,804 non-pistol team-rounds total at least $20,000 (95% match-cluster bootstrap CI 52.5% to 55.7%), 35.5% fall between $10,000 and $19,999 (CI 33.8% to 37.3%), and 10.4% are below $10,000 (CI 9.6% to 11.1%). The non-pistol median is $20,750 (CI $20,400 to $21,000) and the modal $1,000 bin is $21,000 to $21,999 (299 team-rounds). Pistol rounds (1 and 13; 540 team-rounds) all sit below $10,000, with a mean of $3,257 and a maximum of $5,200. The $10,000 and $20,000 lines are UNVERIFIED cut points drawn for reference; nothing here tests an outcome against them.

## Caveats and assumptions

1. **Round count is 2,672, not 2,673.** `rounds.parquet` has 2,673 rounds, but `round_buy_types.parquet` has 2,672. The one missing round is `csds/2026/10/07/22e9c3e7-a333-4ebc-a51c-19ebbcc13ef1/csds`, round 16. At freeze end it has 5 CT and 4 T players, and `alive_gap_snapshots` = 9. I excluded it, so every kept team has 5 players.
2. **Pistol n is 270 rounds, not 300.** The sample holds partial matches: 6 matches have no round 1 and 24 have no round 13, and matches average 17.8 rounds (minimum 2). I did not check why these matches are truncated. The tomes were not used.
3. **Equipment definition.** The team value is the sum of `equipment_value_calc` over each team's players at the first snapshot of the round. The first snapshot has `seconds_since_live` = 0 in every case, and the sums reproduce `ct_equipment` and `t_equipment` in `round_buy_types` exactly (0 mismatches over 2,672 rounds). I did not check the field against CS2 game rules, such as whether it counts armor, helmets, defuser, or grenades.
4. **Cut lines are unverified.** The $10,000 and $20,000 lines are drawn for reference and labelled UNVERIFIED in the legend. A team at exactly $10,000 counts in the $10,000-to-under-$20,000 group.
5. **Intervals.** Percentile 95% intervals from 1,000 match-cluster resamples (`random.Random(0)`, 150 matches drawn with replacement). Each resample keeps every round of a drawn match, so the two sides of a round stay together. The intervals describe this 150-match sample, not the full 1,752-match 2026 tome.
6. **Count versus share.** The bars and bin intervals are counts of team-rounds. Shares and their intervals are given separately in the key numbers table.
7. **Visual check.** I checked the SVG by parsing it (well-formed, 28 text labels as expected), comparing bar heights with the bin counts, and checking label positions by coordinate arithmetic. I did not view it in a rendered browser.
