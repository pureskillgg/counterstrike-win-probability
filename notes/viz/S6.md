# S6: Bunching by side and round outcome

Descriptive, not causal. Source: 1 Hz in-round sample (150 matches, 2,673 rounds, 2026-10 days 01-07).
Files: chart `data\viz\S6.svg`, numbers `data\viz\S6.json`, script `scripts\viz\S6.py`.

## Readout

Bunching is the share of a side's alive players who have a same-side teammate within 500 game units (2D, x/y). It starts near 100% at freeze end for both sides and falls as players spread out and die. The four curves separate after about 15 s. At 30 s, CT-won rounds show 27.7% bunching against 23.2% in CT-lost rounds, and T-won rounds show 55.7% against 48.9% in T-lost rounds. The gap widens after 45 s, but the lost side has far fewer players alive by then (about 1.7 CT players at 60 s in CT-lost rounds, against 2.8 in CT-won rounds). A lone survivor cannot be bunched, so part of the late gap is mechanical. The requested first-30-second mean does not separate outcomes: the early seconds, where both outcomes look the same, dominate the average. The CT won-minus-lost difference is -0.9 points (95% interval -2.0 to +0.3) and the T difference is +0.4 points (-0.8 to +1.8). The round-level correlation between first-30-second mean bunching and winning is r = 0.013 (95% match-cluster interval -0.025 to 0.053) across 5,346 team-rounds, pooled over both sides. Within a side it is 0.037 (-0.004 to 0.079) for CT and 0.086 (0.047 to 0.131) for T. These are associations, not effects.

## Method

- Bunching flag: a player-second is bunched if another alive player on the same side in the same snapshot is within distance 500 (x/y, distance <= 500). Share per side-second = bunched / alive.
- Series: side (CT, T) by whether that side won (ct_win = 1 means CT won).
- Intervals: match-cluster bootstrap. Resample the 150 matches with replacement, 1,000 times, seed 0, pooled ratios recomputed each time.
- Points are drawn only where at least 30 rounds contribute.
- Correlation unit: team-round. Each side in each round gives x = mean of its per-second shares over seconds 0 to 29 (seconds with at least one alive player), y = 1 if that side won.

## Checks run

- No duplicate (match, round, second, player) rows. No nulls in positions.
- Round count from the rounds table matches the CT-won count (1,427).
- Bunching flags verified against a brute-force pairwise distance check on 20,000 random team-seconds: 0 mismatches (34,136 bunched players both ways).
- Every drawn point lies inside its own bootstrap interval; no negative counts; all shares between 0 and 1.
- SVG checked from its text and coordinates (viewBox 800 x 450, tick labels 0 to 90 s and 0 to 100%, legend, n label). The file was not rendered as an image.

## Caveats

1. Descriptive, not causal. The outcome is only known at round end. Economy, buy type, map and site, and round state are not adjusted for.
2. Alive-count mechanics: a side with fewer players alive cannot bunch as much. Late-round gaps, and probably part of the within-side correlations, reflect attrition. No alive-count-stratified analysis was run.
3. The first-30-second window is dominated by the first 15 s, where shares are near 100% at freeze end and the two outcomes look the same. That dilutes the window mean.
4. The pooled r mixes sides. T has higher bunching (team-round mean 0.70 for won and 0.67 for lost, against 0.48 and 0.47 for CT) and a lower win rate, so the pooled r is near zero. The within-side values are more informative but are unadjusted.
5. Only the 500-unit radius is used; it was not varied. z is ignored.
6. The sample is 150 matches from 2026-10 days 01 to 07. The batch effect noted in the handoff (2026-10-08 holds most clean rounds) was not checked separately. The flagged anomaly round (match m_7392c5c6d537a57c2e735e98, round 16) is included and was not tested separately. Overtime rounds (round_number up to 29) are included.
7. Bootstrap: 150 clusters, percentile intervals, 1,000 resamples. Coverage was not checked. Both sides of a round sit in the same match cluster.
8. The series thin after about 60 s; the lost-side series at 90 s rest on 200 to 232 rounds, so their intervals are wide.
9. round_buy_types.parquet has 2,672 rows against 2,673 rounds. It was not used.
10. The share near 1.0 at second 0 is a freeze-end spawn effect, not a finding.
11. Not run: radius sensitivity, alive-count adjustment, batch-stratified checks, exclusion of the anomaly round.
