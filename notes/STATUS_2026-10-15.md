my own concurrent jobs (merge, agents). TabPFN needs ~6-12 GB host memory, TabICL 15 GB+ even on 20,000 rows. The one fine-tune that completed (TabPFN v2, 143k rows, 6 epochs,
GPU peak 5.7 GB, 2,552 s) is in `exp_finetune.json`: +0.0046 log loss vs XGBoost [0.0021, 0.0074]. The new runner serialises with the final chain and samples the GPU every 20 s.
