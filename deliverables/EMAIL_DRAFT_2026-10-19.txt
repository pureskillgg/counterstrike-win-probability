The round win-probability package is ready: the submission model, the score-lead comparison model, one Word report (wrap-up, methodology, deliverable summary, experiment register and presentation list), the slides, and the scoring function with the model files.

Submission model (test: 3,059 matches, 3,956,374 seconds, 2026): 57 inputs (59 minus the heavy-weapon group), no score lead, all round types, one seed. Per-second log loss 0.456411 [0.453311, 0.459138]; validation 0.453557. It needs per-tick player status. Serving parity: the scoring function reproduces the grid's predictions on 294 October test matches (374,500 seconds) with a maximum gap of 0.0001 percentage points.

Comparison (same test seconds): the score-lead comparison model, 58 inputs, mean of five seeds, scores 0.455605. The submission is worse by +0.000806 [+0.000508, +0.001105], which is the cost of leaving out the score lead.
- Event-only model, 14 inputs, for tomes without per-tick player status: 0.498695 on the same seconds. The paired gap is +0.043090 [+0.041449, +0.044884].

Score lead, 2026 data: the score lead changes in every one of the 259,948 consecutive round pairs (282,284 rounds, 14,814 matches). In the 245,136 pairs with no side swap, the change equals the previous round's CT-win sign in every pair; the current round's share there (62.91%) equals how often the same side wins two rounds in a row. Over all pairs, the previous-round share is 96.79% and the current-round share is 62.22%. The earlier quick-check figures (95.13%, 0.37%, 59.93%, 35.58%) did not reproduce and are withdrawn. This is consistent with the score lead being the running score of earlier rounds; it does not show that the score lead contains the current round's result. The reason given for leaving it out was round leakage. [Owner to confirm that reason before this is sent.] Sources: data/combined/exp_scorelead_leak.json and scripts/model/exp_scorelead_leak.py.

Open items, stated plainly:
1. Monotone constraints: the submission keeps them. On the 59-input model it comes from (no score lead, before the heavy-weapon group was dropped), removing them changes test log loss by +0.000092 [+0.000009, +0.000176] (3,059 test matches) and validation log loss by -0.000064 [-0.000183, +0.000066] (1,168 validation matches). A positive test change means removing them makes the test loss worse (data/combined/exp_59_nomono.json). The older 13-input event-only model (winprob_v4_basic, no score lead) had its loss lowered on both splits by removing them (test -0.000443, validation -0.000188; data/combined/exp_mono_paired.json). The 14-input event-only model has not been tested without them.
