WHAT THE DATA IS
- Positions: data/positions_1hz/<date>.parquet (43 days, 2026-09-01 to 2026-10-13).
  Columns: match_key, round, tick, second, player_id, side, x, y, z, yaw. One row per living player per live second.
  Dead players are NOT in these files. The embedding has to handle missing players (mask), per the brief.
- Map per match: data/combined/cache_snapshot_2026.parquet (map_name). 10 matches have no map name; these are the same 10 excluded matches.
- Raw per-tick positions (player_vector) exist only on E: (read-only). The viewer needs them to render any match that is not the sample.

THE VIEWER (csds-viewer-2d)
- Static HTML/JS: open index.html (or serve the folder with python -m http.server for ?match= links).
- Checked: its only network use is reading files in its own folder. No external calls.
- Renders a DSDK match folder (channel files, including player_vector) and overlays a CSV with a tick column (for example our win probability).
- Sample match: csds/2026/10/19/05c94574-b25b-4143-b1f3-7572dd808ba8 (de_mirage).
- Next: build a viewer match folder for one of our matches from the raw player_vector on E:, then overlay the 57 submission's per-second probability.
